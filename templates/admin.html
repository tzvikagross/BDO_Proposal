Services & fees

This is the list of services the wizard offers, and the types of client it offers them for. Each segment becomes a separate fee table in the proposal. For every service, set a default fee in each currency and choose the fee unit. Changes are saved to assets/services/services.json; each save keeps the previous version under assets/services/history.

Types of client

Each type of client has two letters (used in proposal numbers, e.g. LF26-0001), a cover title, a default currency, and its own segments and pre-ticked services in the wizard.

Fee units

The units offered in the drop-down next to every fee.
